About us

Not a chatbot. Real engineers who deploy AI.

We're forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) and experienced technical support engineers. For years we've deployed enterprise software, solved complex technical problems and kept busy professionals productive. Now we bring that to AI.

Most AI tools assume you have time to learn them. Senior people in mechanical, electrical, aviation and marine don't. So we come to you: a video call, a conversation about your day, then a secure remote session where we set everything up on your machine while you watch, and explain each step as we go.

When we're done, you have working automations, and you understand what they do and why.
